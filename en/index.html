Our factory

From steel sheet to finished thresher

Every machine follows the same six-step path inside our Visnagar workshop.

01

Design & drawing

Each model starts as a drawing sized for its crop and drive.

02

Cutting & forming

Steel sheet and sections are cut and bent to size.

03

Welding & frame

Frames, hoppers and chutes are welded into shape.

04

Drum & sieve assembly

The threshing drum, concave and sieves are fitted and balanced.

05

Trial run

Each machine is run before it leaves the workshop.

06

Paint & dispatch

Painted, checked and sent to your village or dealer.

All questions
Which thresher is best for castor?

A castor thresher is built to shell castor capsules cleanly. Share your tractor HP and we will recommend the right model.

Tractor PTO or electric motor?

We build both. Tractor PTO suits field work, an electric motor suits a fixed shed or mill. Tell us which you have.

Can one machine thresh many crops?

Yes. Our multicrop thresher covers wheat, bajra, jowar, gram and soybean by changing the sieves.

Do you supply spare parts?

Yes. Sieves, belts, bearings and drum parts are supplied from the factory in Visnagar.
