Frequently asked questions

Straight answers from the factory. Cannot find yours? Message us on WhatsApp.

Which thresher is best for castor?

A castor thresher is built to shell castor capsules cleanly. Share your tractor HP and we will recommend the right model.

Tractor PTO or electric motor?

We build both. Tractor PTO suits field work, an electric motor suits a fixed shed or mill. Tell us which you have.

Can one machine thresh many crops?

Yes. Our multicrop thresher covers wheat, bajra, jowar, gram and soybean by changing the sieves.

Do you supply spare parts?

Yes. Sieves, belts, bearings and drum parts are supplied from the factory in Visnagar.

Can you match my tractor?

Share your tractor HP and we will set up the machine to suit it.

Do you deliver outside Mehsana?

We serve farmers, dealers and mills across Gujarat. Ask us about your location.

How do I get a quote?

Use the contact form, call, or WhatsApp. Tell us your crop, tractor and village.

Can I visit the factory?

Visitors are welcome. Call first so we can show you the machines working.

Is subsidy available on threshers?

Threshers can be covered by Gujarat mechanisation schemes through the iKhedut portal. Check the current official notice.

Do you issue a GST invoice?

Yes. Ask for a GST tax invoice with your order. Keep it for warranty and subsidy claims.

What warranty do you give?

Warranty terms are set per model. Ask for the written terms with your quote.

How do I maintain my thresher?

Grease bearings, check belts and bolts, clean sieves, and store it covered after the season.
