Spares & service

Keep your thresher working through every season. Ask for parts and service from the factory that built it.

Spare parts we supply

  • Sieves for each crop
  • Belts and pulleys
  • Bearings
  • Drum and concave parts
  • Blower parts
  • Wheels

Service support

  • Help over phone or WhatsApp
  • Pre-season checkup guidance
  • Part replacement advice
  • Factory visit for repairs

Warranty

Warranty terms are set per model. Ask for the written terms with your quote and keep your invoice.

Pre-season checklist

  • Grease all bearings
  • Check belt tension and condition
  • Tighten bolts on drum and frame
  • Clean sieves and screens
  • Check guards before starting
  • Store covered after the season

Request a part or service

Tell us the machine, the part and your village. We reply on WhatsApp.
