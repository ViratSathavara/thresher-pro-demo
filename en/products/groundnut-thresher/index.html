Groundnut Thresher

Our groundnut thresher strips pods from dried vines quickly and keeps the kernels intact. Sieves can be matched to your pod size. It runs on a tractor PTO or an electric motor, and is built in Visnagar, Gujarat.

Key features

  • Strips pods from dried groundnut vines
  • Keeps pods and kernels intact for better value
  • Sieves matched to your pod size
  • Tractor PTO or electric motor drive
  • Wheel-mounted for moving between fields

Questions about this machine

Does it damage the pods?

It is designed to keep pods and kernels intact. Tell us your variety and we will advise.

Can the sieves change?

Yes. We match the sieves to your pod size.

Can I run it on an electric motor?

Yes. Choose PTO or electric motor when you order.
